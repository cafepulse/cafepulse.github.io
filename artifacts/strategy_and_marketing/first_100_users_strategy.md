# CafePulse: Roadmap to the First 100 Users

This document provides a practical, step-by-step guide to acquiring the first 100 active users and customers for CafePulse.

---

## 1. Target Communities & Channels

To acquire users organically, we must target spaces where Indonesian MikroTik admins and RT/RW Net operators seek configuration help:

- **Facebook Groups**:
  - *MikroTik Indonesia* (~200k members)
  - *RT RW NET INDONESIA* (~150k members)
  - *Solusi MikroTik & RT RW NET* (~80k members)
- **Telegram Channels**:
  - *Grup MikroTik Indonesia*
  - *RT/RW Net Sharing Group*
- **Forums & Repositories**:
  - *Kaskus Jaringan* (historical but still visited)
  - *GitHub Jaringan / RouterOS tools lists*

---

## 2. Tactical Execution Plan (Step-by-Step)

### 2.1 Step 1: Solve a Specific Pain Point (Days 1–10)
- **Action**: Share a free script or utility. Do not pitch CafePulse as a commercial tool first.
- **Tactic**: Create a tutorial post: *"How to automatically print vouchers in a grid template without Winbox."* Present CafePulse's Free Edition as a simple utility to do this.
- **Goal**: Secure 10 initial downloads and get basic feedback.

### 2.2 Step 2: Open the Beta Tester Program (Days 11–20)
- **Action**: Invite 10 users to the Beta Tester Program on Facebook and Telegram groups.
- **Tactic**: Pitch the reward: a **1-Year Pro License key** for reporting 3 bugs, and a **5-Year Pro License key** for top contributors.
- **Goal**: Secure active testing configurations and build early community presence on Discord.

### 2.3 Step 3: Launch the Founder Program Pitch (Days 21–40)
- **Action**: Pitch the **100-member limited Founder Program** on Facebook and Telegram.
- **Tactic**: Highlight:
  - Capped strictly at 100 slots.
  - Price: **Rp499.000** (One-Time Purchase).
  - Benefits: Private Discord channel access, input on roadmap, Wall of Founders.
- **Goal**: Acquire the first 30–50 paying Founder users.

### 2.4 Step 4: Leverage Word of Mouth (Days 41–60)
- **Action**: Encourage active Founders to share reviews or screenshots of their setups.
- **Tactic**: Offer referral codes (e.g. affiliate commission or discounts) to Founders who refer other operators.
- **Goal**: Fill the remaining 50 slots to hit the 100 Founder Users milestone.
