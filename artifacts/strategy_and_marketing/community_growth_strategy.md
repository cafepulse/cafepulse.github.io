# CafePulse: Community Growth Strategy

This document details the organic (zero-ad-spend) community scaling strategy for CafePulse, focusing on the Indonesian MikroTik and RT/RW Net markets.

---

## 1. Organic Acquisition Channels

Since CafePulse is a solo developer project, growth relies on adding value to active network administration spaces:

- **Facebook Groups (MikroTik Indonesia)**: Large communities of local technicians and shop owners. Sharing tutorials, config scripts, and operational templates is highly effective.
- **Discord Server**: The central community hub.
- **GitHub Repository**: The development hub. An active GitHub repository builds trust with technical users.
- **Tutorials & Documentation**: Technical guides and troubleshooting walkthroughs that solve common network issues.
- **Showcases**: Sharing UI layouts and live plots to generate interest.

---

## 2. Phased Growth Roadmap

### 2.1 Phase 1: 0 ➔ 10 Users (The Early Adopters)
- **Objective**: Get the first 10 active local installations to validate connection stability.
- **Acquisition Strategy**: Reach out directly to network technicians and local administrators. Share the app as a free utility to troubleshoot DHCP lease tables without accessing Winbox.
- **Action**: Share download links in Indonesian MikroTik Telegram groups and Facebook forums.

### 2.2 Phase 2: 10 ➔ 50 Users (Building Trust)
- **Objective**: Establish community channels and convert early testers into Pro/Founder users.
- **Acquisition Strategy**: Launch the official Discord server. Introduce the **Beta Tester Program** (limited to 10 testers) to source real-world configurations.
- **Action**: Publish the first setup tutorials on YouTube or Facebook showing how CafePulse generates vouchers.

### 2.3 Phase 3: 50 ➔ 100 Users (The Founder Goal)
- **Objective**: Fill the 100 slots in the Founder Program.
- **Acquisition Strategy**: Highlight the **100-member limit** on the website and Discord. Emphasize that Founders get direct input on features.
- **Action**: Share PDF voucher printing templates on local forums to show the value CafePulse adds to businesses.

### 2.4 Phase 4: 100 ➔ 500 Users (Scaling)
- **Objective**: Scale to general commercial licensing.
- **Acquisition Strategy**: Encourage user showcases (e.g., sharing photos of screens running CafePulse). Publish detailed guides on topics like *VLAN configuration* or *routing table monitoring*.
- **Action**: Establish the **Community Advisor Program** to recognize active users, helping delegate support workloads.
