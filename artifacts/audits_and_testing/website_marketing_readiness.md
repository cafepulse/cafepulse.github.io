# CafePulse Website: Marketing & Onboarding Readiness Audit

This report audits the marketing readiness of the V1 copy, checking if it answers the core onboarding questions.

---

## 1. Onboarding Checklist Audit

### 1.1 What is CafePulse?
- **Current Status**: **Fully Answered**.
- **Assessment**: The homepage header clearly identifies it as a *"Local-First MikroTik Operations Platform"*. It defines it as a lightweight desktop tool combining monitoring, management, and voucher generation.

### 1.2 Who is the Target User?
- **Current Status**: **Partially Answered**.
- **Assessment**: Homepage mentions solo developers, network administrators, and coffee shop owners. It should explicitly mention RT/RW Net operators and local hotspot businesses, as defined in the master roadmap.
- **Action**: Add a targeted copy section: *"Designed for: Network Engineers, RT/RW Net Providers, Coffee Shop Owners, and Local Venues."*

### 1.3 What are the Benefits?
- **Current Status**: **Fully Answered**.
- **Assessment**: The copy highlights real-time RouterOS CPU/RAM metrics, interactive DHCP lease tables, and fast PDF voucher exports.

### 1.4 What are the Advantages (Unique Value Prop)?
- **Current Status**: **Fully Answered**.
- **Assessment**: Clearly highlights:
  - *Local-First / Private*: All credentials and logs stored locally in SQLite.
  - *Offline-Friendly*: Fully functional in local intranet setups without internet loops.
  - *No Subscriptions*: A flat perpetual fee of Rp499.000 vs. competitors charging monthly fees.

### 1.5 Why Buy the Professional Edition?
- **Current Status**: **Fully Answered**.
- **Assessment**: Evaluates key features like automated database scheduled backups, white-labeled PDF diagnostic exports, and interactive DHCP controls.

### 1.6 Difference between Free & Pro Editions?
- **Current Status**: **Fully Answered**.
- **Assessment**: The comprehensive comparison grid on `pricing.html` makes the division clear.

### 1.7 What is the Product Philosophy?
- **Current Status**: **Fully Answered**.
- **Assessment**: Explained in `about.html`, emphasizing privacy, independence from venture capital, and local software control.

---

## 2. Recommended Copy Polish
- **Home Hero**: Expand the subtitle to explicitly state: *"Perfect for RT/RW Net providers, local coffee shops, and network technicians."*
- **Pricing Card**: Emphasize the **5-Year Update Entitlement** and write explicitly: *"Software remains functional locally after your entitlement expires. No forced shutdowns or blockages."*
