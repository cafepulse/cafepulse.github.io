# CafePulse Support Policy

CafePulse provides technical support to help users configure, maintain, and troubleshoot the application. Below is the detailed scope, channels, and terms based on the product edition.

---

## 1. Support Channels & Terms

### 1.1 Free Edition
- **Channel:** Documentation, Knowledge Base, GitHub Issues, and Discord Community.
- **Terms:** Voluntary, community-driven support. No guaranteed response times.

### 1.2 Professional Edition
- **Channel:** Official email support at `support@cafepulse.app` (or `support@cafepulse.net`) and dedicated Discord channels.
- **Terms:** **Best Effort Support** only. Response times are not guaranteed due to solo developer limitations, but we aim to address structural bugs and activation issues as a priority.

---

## 2. Scope of Support

### 2.1 What is Covered (In-Scope)
- Installing and upgrading CafePulse on supported operating systems (Windows 10/11, standard Linux distros).
- Software-level bugs, UI crashes, or database lock errors.
- Issues related to license key activation or transfer.
- Failure of CafePulse to fetch data via RouterOS API (provided API credentials and port config are correct).

### 2.2 What is NOT Covered (Out-of-Scope)
- **MikroTik Router Configuration:** We do not configure firewall rules, routing protocols, VLANs, or VPNs on your MikroTik hardware.
- **OS Troubleshooting:** Resolving general issues with your host operating system, local network interfaces, or anti-virus blocks.
- **Legacy Systems:** Support for hardware/firmware versions marked as deprecated or unsupported.

---

## 3. Submitting a Support Ticket

When submitting a ticket, please include:
- Your license key or Order ID.
- The compiled diagnostic file or logs (found in `logs/`).
- Details of the issue (reproduction steps, router model, and firmware version).
