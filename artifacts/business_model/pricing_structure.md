# CafePulse Pricing Structure

CafePulse offers flexible, developer-friendly pricing options designed to match your network operations. All tiers are based on our local-first, privacy-respecting architecture.

---

## 2. Professional License Terms
- A Professional license key is **perpetual** for the version purchased.
- Licensing is workstation-locked. One license key allows active activation on exactly **1 PC** owned by the licensed user.
- **Activation Modes**: Supports both Online Activation and Offline Activation (using machine-bound `.licreq` files) to accommodate air-gapped network installations.
- **5-Year Update Entitlement**: The license includes 5 years of minor and major software updates.
- **Local Persistence Guarantee**: The software operates fully locally offline and remains fully functional after the 5-year update entitlement window expires.
