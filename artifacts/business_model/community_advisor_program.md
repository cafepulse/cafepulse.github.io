# CafePulse Community Advisor Program

The **Community Advisor Program** is designed to empower and reward active members of the CafePulse community who assist others, create tutorials, and represent the product on public forums.

---

## 1. Role of a Community Advisor
Community Advisors are senior network engineers, IT consultants, or passionate users who:
- Assist new users with configuration queries on Discord, Reddit, and GitHub.
- Write guides, record video tutorials, or publish articles on setting up CafePulse with MikroTik.
- Help moderate community forums and chat channels.
- Participate in feedback and roadmap discussions to help guide product development.

---

## 2. Who is Eligible?
We select advisors based on active contributions:
- Answering questions on community hubs.
- Creating open-source scripts or templates for CafePulse integration.
- Demonstrating a deep understanding of network security, MikroTik setups, and local monitoring.

---

## 3. Advisor Privileges
To show our gratitude, Community Advisors receive:
- **Complementary Professional License:** Standard Professional license key with a 5-Year Update Entitlement (local persistence guaranteed: the core software remains fully active locally offline after the entitlement expires).
- **Advisor Status:** A custom role and badge on Discord, GitHub, and the CafePulse app.
- **Preview Builds:** Immediate access to early architectural designs and drafts.
- **Beta Seat:** Automatic enrollment in the private beta circle.

---

## 4. How to Apply
Applications are reviewed semi-annually. You can apply or nominate a community member by sending details of their contributions to:
- **Email:** community@cafepulse.com
