## UNIT ECONOMICS ANALYSIS

### Revenue per License

| Komponen | Nilai |
|---|---|
| Harga Jual | Rp 499.000 |
| Payment Gateway Fee (2.5%) | - Rp 12.475 |
| Affiliate Commission (jika ada, 10%) | - Rp 49.900 |
| **Gross Revenue per License** | **Rp 436.625** |
| **Gross Margin** | **~87.5%** |

### Break-Even Analysis

| Skenario | Lisensi Terjual | Revenue | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum Viable | 50 | Rp 24.950.000 | Cover 3 bulan operasional |
| Target Launch | 100 | Rp 49.900.000 | Cover 6 bulan + investasi lanjutan |
| Growth Target | 600 | Rp 299.400.000 | Sustain full-time development |
| Scale Target | 2.000 | Rp 998.000.000 | Expand team, new features |
