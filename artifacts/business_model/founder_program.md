# CafePulse: Founder Program Final Policy & Guidelines

This document establishes the final operational parameters, pricing structures, limits, and legal boundaries of the CafePulse Founder Program.

---

## 1. Legal Boundaries & Participant Role

To avoid regulatory issues, the Founder Program is governed by these legal definitions:

- **Early Adopters / Supporters**: Founders are early supporters who purchase the software to fund early desktop developments.
- **No Equity / Shares**: Membership grants **zero corporate shares, zero business equity, and zero company ownership**.
- **No Revenue Sharing**: Founders are not entitled to commissions, profit-sharing, or dividend distributions.
- **No Partnership Status**: Founders are customers, not commercial business partners or legal agents of the developer.

---

## 2. Program Boundaries & Pricing System

- **Membership Cap**: Limited strictly to the first **100 registered members**. Once 100 license keys are activated, the Founder program tier closes permanently.
- **Software Tier**: Professional Edition.
- **Pricing**: **Rp499.000** (One-Time Purchase).
- **Seat Limit**: **1 License = 1 PC** (active activation locked).
- **Support Entitlement**: **5-Year Update Entitlement**.
  - Includes all minor and major updates for 5 years.
  - **Local Persistence Guarantee**: The software operates fully locally offline and remains fully functional after the 5-year entitlement window expires.

---

## 3. Supporter Benefits Matrix

Founders are entitled to:
1. **Early Access Builds**: Priority testing builds featuring experimental modules (e.g. smart RouterOS config viewers).
2. **Direct Developer Channel**: A private channel in the official Discord server to suggest feature additions.
3. **Founder Recognition**: Optional listing on the official "Wall of Founders" in the website/app about panel.
4. **App Badge**: A custom "Founder" status badge next to the license verification key inside the app.

---

## 4. Copy Guidelines (Prohibited Terms)
- **DO NOT USE**: `Lifetime Updates`, `Lifetime License`, `Lifetime Membership`, `Lifetime Support`, `Shareholder`, `Investment`.
- **USE ONLY**: `5-Year Update Entitlement`, `Founder Supporter`, `One-time Purchase`, `Local Persistence`.
