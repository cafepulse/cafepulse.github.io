# Refund Policy for CafePulse

**Last Updated: June 2, 2026**

Thank you for choosing CafePulse. We stand behind our software and want you to be satisfied with your purchase.

---

## 1. 30-Day Evaluation Policy

Because CafePulse offers a comprehensive Free Edition and demo environments, we strongly encourage all users to download, install, and test the software for compatibility with their hardware and MikroTik configurations **before purchasing a Professional Edition license**.

---

## 2. Refund Eligibility

We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** under the following conditions:
- The refund request is made within **30 days** of the initial purchase date.
- You have encountered technical difficulties or compatibility issues that our support team was unable to resolve within 7 business days of your report.
- You agree to deactivate and uninstall the license key from your system. Upon refund approval, the license key will be blacklisted on our validation servers.

---

## 3. Non-Refundable Items

We cannot issue refunds for:
- Requests made after the **30-day window** has elapsed.
- Incompatibility issues with unsupported legacy versions of RouterOS (below version 6.x) that are explicitly documented as unsupported.
- Keys purchased through unauthorized third-party resellers (please contact the reseller directly).

---

## 4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email our billing department:
- **Email:** billing@cafepulse.com
- **Subject:** Refund Request - [Your License Key / Order ID]
- **Body:** Please specify the reason for the refund, and attach any crash logs or details about the technical issues you faced.

Approved refunds will be credited back to your original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
