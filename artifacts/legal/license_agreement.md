## 4. Hardware Signature & Activation

To verify compliance with the 1 PC seat limit without compromising user privacy, CafePulse utilizes a local machine-bound cryptographic signature. License checks use a hashed hardware signature matching node, host OS, and system parameters. This hash is validated locally offline or during the one-time activation process, respecting local-first data privacy rules. No continuous telemetry or active tracking of network traffic is performed by CafePulse.
