CafePulse Beta Program

Help us stress-test our RouterOS integrations, OS setups, and UI performance in diverse environments.

Release Channels

Alpha Branch: Weekly builds with experimental features. Recommended only for test rigs.

Beta Branch: Monthly releases undergoing final stability checks. Suitable for regular network audits.

Reporting Guidelines

1. **Get crash logs:** Copy trace files from `logs/crash/` or general logs inside `logs/`.
2. **Detail steps:** Outline steps to reproduce the issue.
3. **Hardware info:** Model of your MikroTik router, RouterOS version, and client count.

Tester Rewards

The Beta Tester Program has a strict cap of 10 active Beta Testers. Candidates are rewarded based on validated reports:

• Top Contributor: 5-Year Professional License (for uncovering structural bugs, database lockups, or multithreading crashes).
• Contributor: 1-Year Professional License (for documenting and submitting at least 3 validated bugs with system parameters).
• Exclusive Beta Tester badge inside the app.

Submit a Bug Report

Reports are directly dispatched to our engineering desk at cafepulse.network@gmail.com.
