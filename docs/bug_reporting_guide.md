# Bug Reporting Guide

Welcome to the CafePulse Bug Reporting Guide. Your feedback is crucial for improving the stability and performance of the CafePulse network operations platform. Please follow the instructions below to submit a bug report.

## How to Report a Bug

We use Google Forms for our primary Beta Tester intake and Bug Reporting, but you can also send bug reports directly via email.

1. **Google Form:** Open the [Beta Tester & Bug Report Form](https://forms.gle/VPwQ3jRBySbCEvKX7) and fill out the details.
2. **Direct Email:** If you prefer email, or if you need to attach larger files that the form does not support, you can send an email directly to `cafepulse.network@gmail.com`.

## What to Include in Your Report

A good bug report helps our engineering team replicate and fix the issue faster. Please include:

1. **Bug Description**: A clear and concise description of what the bug is.
2. **Steps to Reproduce**: Detailed steps on how to trigger the bug.
   - *Example:* "1. Open Profile tab, 2. Enter IP address, 3. Click 'Save', 4. App crashes."
3. **Expected Behavior**: What you expected to happen.
4. **Actual Behavior**: What actually happened.
5. **System Information**:
   - Host OS (e.g., Windows 10, Ubuntu 22.04)
   - MikroTik RouterOS version
   - CafePulse version
6. **Error Logs**: Always include relevant crash logs.
   - Logs can be found in your local CafePulse directory under `logs/` and `logs/crash/`.

## Uploading Images and Videos

Visual evidence is extremely helpful for debugging UI or interaction issues.

- **Using Google Forms**: You can upload images directly into the Google Form. 
- **Using Email**: If you are emailing us, you can simply attach the `.png`, `.jpg`, or `.mp4` files to the email.
- **Large Video Files**: If your video recording is larger than standard email attachment limits (usually 25MB):
  1. Upload the video to a cloud storage service like Google Drive, Dropbox, or unlisted YouTube.
  2. Ensure the link permissions are set to "Anyone with the link can view".
  3. Paste the link directly into the Google Form or your email body.

> [!WARNING]
> **Privacy Reminder**: Please ensure that your screenshots or videos do not contain sensitive credentials, public WAN IPs, or private client data unless absolutely necessary. We recommend blurring passwords or sensitive MAC addresses before sharing visual evidence.
